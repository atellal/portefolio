01About

Bridging fundamental physics and technological innovation — from Maxwell's equations to devices you can fabricate.

I am a physicist with a PhD in radiation physics (USTHB, Algiers, 2019) and over five years of postdoctoral research across leading French laboratories — the ENS de Lyon and the Institute of Nanotechnologies of Lyon (INL–CNRS).

My work sits at the meeting point of numerical simulation, micro/nano-fabrication, and advanced lithography. I design and optimise photonic nanostructures, model light–matter interaction with finite-element methods, and translate those designs into real structures through holographic projection and two-photon polymerisation.

Along the way I have contributed to European and ANR-funded projects, authored peer-reviewed publications in nanophotonics, and co-invented an international patent on high-resolution 3D printing. I work in the space where curiosity, precision, and collaboration meet.
